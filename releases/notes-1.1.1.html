Sono 1.1.1

The local model download no longer looks broken.

  • Choosing the local model showed "Downloading, 0%" and never moved, for the entire download. It was working the whole time, but there was no way to tell, and twenty minutes of a frozen zero looks exactly like a hang.
  • The percentage came from a progress figure that does not track the large weights file. It is gone. The row now shows a moving bar and says "Downloading, a few minutes", which is the truth.

Nothing else changed. If the local model already finished downloading, this update does not affect you.
