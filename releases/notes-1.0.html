Sono 1.0

The first release. Dictation that runs on your Mac.

What it does

  • Hold Option and speak, and it stops the moment you let go. Or tap Option to start, tap again to stop. Either way the text lands in whatever app you were already typing in.
  • Transcription runs entirely on your Mac using NVIDIA's Parakeet model. No audio leaves the machine, and it works with the Wi-Fi off.
  • Optional Apple Intelligence cleanup removes fillers, follows your self-corrections, and turns spoken lists into real ones. It ships switched off, so your first dictation is as fast as the machine can go. Turn it on in Settings.
  • A dashboard showing what you dictated, how long it took, and which apps you use it in.

Worth knowing

  • Sono asks for Microphone and Accessibility at first launch. Accessibility is what lets it paste into other apps; without it, text goes to your clipboard instead.
  • Your clipboard is put back the way it was after each paste, images and formatting included.
  • Silence is trimmed before transcription, so pauses at either end cost you nothing.
  • Sono is free. No account, no licence, no expiry.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence cleanup needs a Mac that supports it and works best on shorter dictations; Sono falls back to the raw transcript when it cannot run.
